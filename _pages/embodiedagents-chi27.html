Abstract

Agents are about to jump off our screens, taking on physical bodies to sense and act in the world around us. They start to span an emerging ecosystem of embodiments, from robots to bodily-integrated and environmentally-integrated actuation, making the agent’s body itself an open design variable. As such embodied agents begin to share our daily lives, it is a crucial time to ask: Which bodies, with which capabilities, fit which everyday tasks and needs? What interactions and systems let them behave intelligently around people? And how do we study whether people can live comfortably with them? These threads must advance hand in hand. As interest grows across our CHI community, it is crucial to connect research areas and build the shared ground to develop deeper insights about situated fit of embodiment form, the capabilities they can offer, and how to interact with them. We aim to map the design space, methods, and challenges ahead. This workshop convenes researchers across CHI, from tangible and actuated interfaces, human-robot interaction, and human-AI interaction to design and behavioral science, to build that shared ground. Through in-person talks, discussions anchored in participants’ scenarios, and collective synthesis, participants will seed a design space and name grand challenges for embodied agents in everyday life, and form the community to take them on.
